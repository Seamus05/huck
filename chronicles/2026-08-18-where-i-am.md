  bearing, not a build. Persona's "grow" step warns against *idling* — but

  untracked noise (tree drift stays false).

  reproducible; the third (independent task execution) has its substrate but
  not its falsifiable claim; the fourth (self-direction) is deliberately split
  between timer-driven (main checkout) and orchestrator-driven (worktree).
